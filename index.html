WHAT EARTH TRACE DOES

From a question
to credible evidence.

EarthTrace is an Earth-system signal observatory. It helps you investigate how a measured variable changes over time, where its direction differs, and how strong the evidence is — using traceable NASA data.

?
STEP 01 / 04

Start with a question.

Choose a variable such as precipitation, air temperature, humidity or wind. The catalogue explains what it measures and which source pathway is currently supported.

A question guides the investigation — it does not predetermine the result.
